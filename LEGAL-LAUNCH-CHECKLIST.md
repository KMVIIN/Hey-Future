# Legal launch checklist (France/EU)

The code contains Future-specific legal/privacy/terms/safety pages, but publishing pages does not by itself guarantee legal compliance. Before accepting customers, complete the public identity values in Vercel Environment Variables:

- LEGAL_CONTACT_EMAIL
- PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL
- COMPANY_REGISTERED_ADDRESS
- COMPANY_REGISTRATION_NUMBER
- PUBLICATION_DIRECTOR

Also verify current hosting details, consumer checkout disclosures, VAT/tax handling, cancellation/withdrawal rules, processor agreements, international-transfer safeguards, retention periods, rights-request process, and cookie consent if any non-essential trackers are enabled. Have a France/EU-qualified professional review the final commercial terms.
