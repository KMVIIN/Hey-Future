# Future 4.2 Final Launch changes

- Search & Discover and Map & Places are side-by-side on desktop (50/50).
- Map has its own independent place search.
- Sidebar is scrollable and includes Main/More scroll tabs.
- Free/guest users can choose Lavender, Dark or Sky themes.
- Paid users can customize dashboard density/layout and apply preset or uploaded backgrounds to the whole page or calendar.
- Future-specific Legal notice, Privacy, Terms and Alerts & safety pages added.
- Admin dashboard includes customer economics plus a plan/feature matrix.
- Guest mode remains available without login; cloud/connected/billing features require an account.
